\skel{[P1 DIAGNOSIS]}\; \lipsum[7-9]

% 문단 2 · Measurement + 계보(token bucket / credit-based)
\skel{[P2 MEASUREMENT + LINEAGE]}\; We introduce completed GPU service $S(t)$. The controller is
a token bucket whose tokens are minted by \emph{completed GPU service} rather than by
wall-clock time, which makes it credit-based rather than rate-based: view growth paces itself
to whatever the hardware actually finished (Fig.~\ref{fig:growthtrace}). \lipsum[10-12]

% 문단 3 · Mechanism
\skel{[P3 MECHANISM]}
\begin{align}
  A^{*}(t) &= \min\!\Big(M(t),\; A_0 + \Big\lfloor \tfrac{B_0+\gamma S(t)}{\kappa} \Big\rfloor \Big) \label{eq:astar}\\
  B_t &= B_0 + \gamma S(t) - \kappa\big(|A_t| - A_0\big) \;\ge\; 0 \label{eq:bucket}
\end{align}
\lipsum[13-14]

% 문단 4 · Correction rule + Forward pointer. carry vs no-prepurchase를 여기서 두 문장으로 종결.
\skel{[P4 CORRECTION + FWD]}\; After prunes and removals the realized growth is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:quota}
  Q_t = \min\!\big(|C_t|,\; [\,A^{*}(t) - |A_{t^-}|\,]_+\big).
\end{equation}
\pend{close carry vs.\ no-prepurchase here, in two sentences}
\S\ref{sec:exp} shows that this law holds with zero integer error, and how quality degrades
gracefully as $\kappa$ is lowered.

% 문단 5 · run-in. 기여 아님. +0.35%라는 작은 이득 자체를 설계 근거로 쓴다.
\noindent\textbf{Slot placement.}\;
\skel{[P5 RUN-IN, NOT A CONTRIBUTION]}\; Placing the opened quota along the trajectory by
temporal maximin and interval water filling gains only $+0.35\%$. The bottleneck is therefore
\emph{how many} views can be admitted, not \emph{which} ones \textemdash\ which is why this
subsection concentrates on growth.

\subsection{Entropy-Regularized Count Balancing}
\label{ssec:ercb}

% 문단 1 · Roadmap(열거형) — chen2026cover §4 도입 형식
\skel{[P1 ROADMAP-ENUM]}\; The derivation proceeds in three steps: (1) expressing
selection-count imbalance as a potential $\Phi$; (2) solving an entropy-regularized Lagrangian
for the unique distribution; (3) extending it to a $K$-view without-replacement block.

% 문단 2 · Formulating
\skel{[P2 FORMULATING]}
\begin{equation}\label{eq:phi}
  \Phi(\mathbf{n}) = \tfrac12\sum_i (n_i-\bar n)^2,
  \qquad
  \Delta_i\Phi = n_i - \bar n + \tfrac12\big(1-\tfrac1N\big).
\end{equation}
The last term is common to all $i$ and does not affect the $\arg\max$. \lipsum[15-16]

% 문단 3 · Deriving — 유일해임을 명시
\skel{[P3 DERIVING]}\; The entropy-regularized Lagrangian admits a unique solution:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:gibbs}
  p_t(i) = \frac{\exp(-\beta n_i)}{\sum_{j}\exp(-\beta n_j)},\qquad \beta = 1/\tau.
\end{equation}
\lipsum[17-19]

% 문단 4 · Extension + 가정 원장 (근사마다 조건을 붙인다 — chen2026cover §4.4 형식)
\skel{[P4 EXTENSION + ASSUMPTION LEDGER]}\; We extend to a $K$-view sequential weighted draw
without replacement, holding the count snapshot $n_i^{(b)}$ fixed within a block
\emph{(a first-order approximation inside the block)}. The conditional entropy retained is
measured by
\begin{equation}\label{eq:rhoH}
  \rho_H = \frac{\sum_{b,k} H(p_{b,k})}{\sum_{b,k}\log|W_b^{(k)}|}.
\end{equation}

% 문단 5 · Scope guard + 계보 3문장 + in-method 실패
\skel{[P5 SCOPE GUARD + LINEAGE + FAILURE]}\; With $\beta=0$ and $K \ge N_t$, the procedure
reduces to random reshuffling over the admitted set. We do not inherit its convergence
guarantees: our set grows during training and the objective is non-convex. The $K$-view draw
is sequential weighted sampling without replacement, i.e.\ a Plackett--Luce draw. Count-based
priorities of this form appear in experience replay; our objective is the opposite of
loss-prioritized data selection, since we equalize exposure rather than concentrate it.
\pend{[in-method failure]} This objective equalizes selection counts but does not equalize
lifetime exposure (\S\ref{sec:exp}); using counts alone as the potential is a design choice
and its limit.
