\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
In this paper, we introduce the closed-form $n^{\text{th}}$ moment of conics and prove that the centroid of a distorted ellipse is expressed by a linear combination of the original moments. Based on these results, we developed an unbiased estimator of circular patterns under distortion for camera calibration. Using this estimator, circular pattern-based calibration overcomes its algorithmic limitation and outperforms checkerboard-based calibration.
