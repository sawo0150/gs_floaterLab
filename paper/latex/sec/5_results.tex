% ---------- 도입 = Setup (번호 없음) ----------
\noindent\textbf{Datasets.}\;
\skel{SETUP 1}\; aria1253, aria1253rot, 301\_305, 12F.
\lipsum[1]

\noindent\textbf{Streaming contract.}\;
\skel{SETUP 2 \textemdash\ OUR CONTRACT LIVES HERE}\;
To evaluate the strict streaming setting, every method receives the same sensor trace
(RGB\,+\,IMU only, no post-processed trajectory or depth), the same $1.5\times$ wall-clock
budget, and performs \emph{zero optimizer updates after the last sensor frame}.
Poses and depths are the online-estimated ones. Results with a post-stream refinement tail
are in the supplementary material.

\noindent\textbf{Metrics.}\;
\skel{SETUP 3 \textemdash\ QUALITY vs.\ RESOURCE GROUPS}\;
Quality: held-out PSNR / SSIM / LPIPS and region-GT AUC/AP.
Resources: wall-time, streaming updates, total admissions, \emph{peak} and final model size.
\pend{define $\rho_H$ here \textemdash\ moved out of \S\ref{ssec:ercb}; it is a metric, not a method}
All numbers are mean\,$\pm$\,std over three runs.
Held-out views are excluded from all supervision for every method; consequently, numbers
reported under a post-processed-pose protocol \emph{are not directly comparable to ours}.

\noindent\textbf{Baselines.}\;
\skel{SETUP 4}\; fixed-FPS dense / random full-pool / covisibility window /
novelty-first / residual-first / clustering-based batch sampler.
\lipsum[2]
