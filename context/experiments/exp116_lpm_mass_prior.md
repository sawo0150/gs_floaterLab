# Exp116 — LPM significant-zone mass prior

Date: 2026-09-24

## Method

Exp115's `1+e_i` prior was safe but inactive. Exp116 removes that arbitrary
unit uniform mass and uses the official LPM 16x16 patch scale directly:

\[
q_i=\frac{a_i+256}{H_iW_i+256},\qquad
p_i\propto q_i\exp[-16n_i/(T+1)],
\]

where `a_i` is the number of pixels covered by significant LPM zones. The 256
pixel pseudocount is exactly one LPM patch. It is common, resolution-aware,
strictly positive, and introduces no learned, scene-specific, or PSNR-tuned
coefficient. The normalized-variance count energy is unchanged.

Evidence is generated by the downloaded LPM author operation at commit
`7c060267`, staged after successful Adam, and published only with the queue's
transactional service commit. Full LightGlue/triangulation and any extra
render, Adam step, or mutation remain absent.

## Verdict

**PASS as an active, quality-safe development composition.** Unlike Exp115,
the combined normalized arm changes actual view service while preserving
quality far inside the -0.5 dB stop line. It does **not** establish a quality
gain: -0.009/+0.003 dB are noise-level. The formula is now frozen for an
untuned RPNG table_01 and Aria1253 transfer. A full 17-scene panel remains
premature unless the transfer is safe and provides stronger causal evidence
than merely nonzero trace changes.
