  \caption{Overview of online Gaussian mapping.
  The frontend supplies camera poses and keyframe geometry priors.
  (a) Optimization-Guided View Growth retains admitted views and expands
  the training set according to completed mapping updates.
  (b) Entropy-Regularized View Sampling favors under-selected views while
  retaining stochasticity; sampled views are used in consecutive updates.
  (c) Ray-space geometry supervision complements the base mapping losses.
  Images, map renders, and estimated keyframe poses are from Aria 1253;
  the camera frusta use estimated orientations with a display-only size.
  The shared map is an external cutaway of the C07 region (reference UID 786),
  with spatial clipping for visualization only. The RGB, depth, and normal
  examples use C10 (UID 1091) and the uncut saved map.
  Pool stages,
  selection counts, probabilities, and the ray illustration are schematic.
  The illustrative saved map was obtained without the ray-space loss
  and is not evidence of that loss's effect.}
