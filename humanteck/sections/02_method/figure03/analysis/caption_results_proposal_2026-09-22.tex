\section{Results and Conclusion}

We evaluate mapping quality on RPNG~\cite{chen2023monocular},
UTMM~\cite{sun2024mm3dgs}, and our Aria sequences, with Carve disabled.
Using shared tracker outputs and matched total training-render budgets,
our method improves scene-averaged held-out PSNR over
VIGS-SLAM~\cite{zhu2026vigs} by 1.60, 0.52, and 2.45\,dB, respectively.
Figure~\ref{fig:photometric_convergence}(a) tracks mean PSNR over
555 fixed held-out views on \texttt{table\_06}.
Among the evaluated checkpoints, ours first exceeds the baseline's highest
observed PSNR (23.28\,dB) at 1,400 mapping iterations, compared with
2,400 for the baseline.
At 1,400 iterations, the selected view in
Fig.~\ref{fig:photometric_convergence}(b) shows clearer lettering than the baseline.

We present an online Gaussian mapping framework that combines
optimization-guided view-set growth and entropy-regularized view sampling
with an opacity-directed Carve loss.
This framework provides a basis for incremental map refinement in robots
and wearable augmented-reality systems under limited computation.
